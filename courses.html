Become a Certified Workplace Strategist

Learn evidence based principles and practical tools to create effective workplace strategies - online or in Stockholm

Why Take This Course ?

This course has been created by Dr Aram Seddigh (PhD), a leading expert in workplace strategy and organisational change. His doctorate on how office types and working methods affect employees’ health and productivity gives him a unique combination of scientific insight and practical experience.

For more than a decade, he has supported private and public organisations in transforming their workplaces and adopting new ways of working. With a background as both an economist and a licensed psychologist, Dr Seddigh brings a holistic understanding of how people, space, technology and leadership shape workplace performance.

His expertise provides the foundation for this course, ensuring an evidence-based and highly practical learning experience.

Course Creator

This course has been created by Dr Aram Seddigh (PhD), a leading expert in the field of workplace strategy and organisational change. With a doctorate focused on how different office types and working methods influence employees’ health and productivity, Dr Seddigh brings a rare combination of scientific insight and hands-on experience.

For more than a decade, he has helped private and public organisations transform their workplaces and successfully transition to new ways of working. His work bridges research, psychology and business strategy — giving course participants access to proven methods that truly make a difference.

In addition to his PhD, Dr Seddigh is both an economist and a licensed psychologist, providing a uniquely holistic perspective on how people, space, technology and leadership shape workplace performance. Course literature

In this book, Aram Seddigh presents a practical, research-based guide to creating and implementing workplace strategies that meet the diverse needs of employees. It combines a solid theoretical foundation with hands-on tools, real examples and approaches that can be tailored to different organisational contexts.

The book is suitable for professional development, higher education and anyone working with workplace transformation. It provides clear methods for designing strategies that support both employee experience and business objectives, and it complements the tools and training used throughout the Workplace Strategist Certification Programme.

“Workplace Strategy for the Flexible Office” is included as core course literature in the programme.

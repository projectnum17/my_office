Open applications welcome

There are currently no open roles, but we’re always curious about people who share our passion for workplace strategy, design, and change. Send us your CV and a short note about what you’d like to do to info@weoffice.se, and we’ll keep your application in mind for future opportunities.

Open vacancies

  • Business Analyst

    • Location

      Remote

    • Experience

      1+ year

    • Work conditions

      Full time, 8-hour work day

    • Description

      Are you a Business Analyst? If so, you have the opportunity to join a dynamic and forward-thinking company that is preparing for international expansion.

      For over a decade, we have been working innovatively and evidence-based to create “More than just beautiful offices” for our clients. Over the past year, we have started a transformation and shifted our business model to scale our services globally.

      We are now looking for a Business Analyst who will take a central role in the company by contributing to the development of our digital services, playing a key role in visualizing business information, and being responsible for the daily operation of our digital platforms.

      With us, you will be part of a multidisciplinary and collaborative team that enjoys challenges and celebrates successes as much as we learn from failures. You will have strong influence and the ability to propose and drive your own ideas forward. You now have the chance to join us at an early stage of our journey, giving you excellent opportunities to grow together with the company’s development and expansion.

      As a company, we believe in continuous learning and development and value creativity. So if you are looking for a role with great variety and strong growth potential, we look forward to receiving your application.

    • About the role

      To succeed in this role, you should be independent and eager to learn and develop new skills. You likely have a background as a consultant. You need to be a “doer” who gets things done and easily finds practical solutions to challenges. Since you will work closely with the whole team, strong communication skills and experience working in cross-functional environments are essential.

      Our office is located in central Stockholm on Kungsgatan, which will also be your primary workplace.

    • Required skills

      SQL/MySQ, ETL development (Extract Transform Load), APIs and integrations, Tableau, Knowledge of GDPR, Broad expertise in the BI/analytics area (at least 3 years)

    • Nice-to-have

      Experience working with IoT data and streaming data, Experience with Azure/GCP/AWS, Proficiency in JavaScript, Experience with SPSS, Basic web development skills, Experience with BI tools other than Tableau (e.g. Power BI, QlikSense, Looker), WordPress, Programming in WordPress using PHP

    • We offer

      • Flexible 8-hours working day
      • Communication window 11:00 - 17:00 (GMT +3)
      • Interesting and ambitious projects
      • Friendly team & and cozy atmosphere
      • Grows and carrier opportunities

About Us

We focus on creating better workplaces with a focus on offices and working methods by planning the physical environment, developing the digital workplace and working with behavioral change.

We do this by developing a workplace strategy, conducting a location search, negotiating rent, developing drawings and interior design concepts, project managing the work to goals and working with change management to achieve desirable behaviors.

  • Our work is grounded in evidence-based and well-proven methods that ensure quality and measurable results.

  • With long-standing experience across sectors – from public institutions and education to corporate and non-profit organisations – we understand the unique challenges of every workplace.

  • Headquartered in Sweden, we collaborate with clients throughout Europe to create workplaces that truly work for people and organisations alike.
